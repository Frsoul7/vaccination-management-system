#UC009 - Vaccination Process Management

#1. Engineering Requirements

### Brief Format
The center coordinator monitors the vaccination process of a vaccination center. The center coordinator requests information
about statistics and charts to the system. The system validates the request and presents the
required data. With this data, the center coordinator can evaluate the performance of the 
vaccination process, and request to the system to generate reports. The system devolves the report in accordance
with the specifications asked by the center coordinator. Furthermore, the center coordinator can analyze
data from other centers, including data from law systems.

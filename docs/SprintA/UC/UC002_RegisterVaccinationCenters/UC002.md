# UC002 - Register Vaccination Centers

## 1. Engineering Requirements

### Brief format


The administrator starts the registration of a new vaccination center. The system requests
the required data and the type of center (health care center or massive vaccination center).
The administrator inserts the required information according to the data fields.
The system checks and presents the data to the administrator and asks him to confirm it.
Upon the administrator confirmation, the system records the data
and informs about the success of the operation.

# Glossary

**Terms, Expressions and Acronyms (TEA) must be organized alphabetically.**

| **_TEA_** (EN)  | **_TEA_** (PT) | **_Description_** (EN)                                           |                                       
|:------------------------|:-----------------|:--------------------------------------------|
| **Administrator** | **Administrador** | **Responsible person for properly configuring and managing core information, essential for the app to be daily operated by the individuals: SNS Users, nurses, receptionists, etc**|
| **Adverse reaction** | **Reação adversa** | **Unexpected or unintended effect of vaccine on SNS user during the recovery period**|
| **Age group** | **Grupo etário** | **Division in groups according to the age of individuals**|
| **AGES** | **AGES** | **Acronym for Agrupamentos de Centros de Saúde**|
| **ARS** | **ARS** | **Acronym for Administração Regional de Saúde**|
| **Center coordinator** | **Coordenador do centro** | **Person responsible to coordinate and monitor a specific Vaccination Center (one coordinator per vaccination center)**|
| **Coronavirus** | **Coronavírus** | **Name given to an extense family of virus which resemble each other**|
| **Covid-19** | **Covid-19** | **Acute respiratory infection caused by the coronavirus SARS-COV-2, potentially serious, highly transmissible and globally distributed**|
| **DGS** | **DGS** | **Acronym for Direção-Geral de Saúde**|
| **Dose** | **Dose** | **Single take of a specific vaccine by a SNS user**|
| **EU COVID Digital Certificate** | **Certificado Digital Europeu Covid** | **Certificate which proves that an SNS User has been administered with a vaccine. It presents which vaccine was taken (brand), the vaccine administration's date and the certificate's expiration date**|
| **Health care center** | **Centro de saúde** | **Basic unit of SNS to attend and provide a wide range of healthcare services to citizens in a certain area**|
| **Immunization** | **Imunização** | **Action of making a individual immune to infection, typically by inoculation**|
| **Nurse** | **Enfermeira/o** | **Person enrolled in the vaccination process, for example is responsible to administer the vaccines to SNS Users**|
| **Pandemic** | **Pandemia** | **Occurring over a wide geographic area**|
| **Receptionist** | **Rececionista** | **Person enrolled in the vaccination process, for example, by being responsible to register SNS Users upon arrival to the vaccination center**|
| **Recovery period** | **Período de recuperação** | **Time that an individual should wait after taking a vaccine, to assure they are no immediate negative reactions**|
| **Recovery room** | **Sala de recuperação** | **Area where the SNS Users recover after the being administered with the vaccine**|
| **SARS-COV-2** | **SARS-COV-2** | **Virus from the coronavirus family, which causes the disease Covid-19 when infects humans**|
| **Scheduled day** | **Dia agendado** | **Day that the SNS user scheduled to take the vaccine**|
| **Slot** | **Janela** | **Period of time which comprises the capacity of one vaccination center to administer the vaccine to a limit number of SNS Users**|
| **SMS** | **SMS** | **Acronym for Short Message Service**|
| **SNS** | **SNS** | **Acronym for Serviço Nacional de Saúde**|
| **SNS user** | **Utente do SNS** | **Person registered in the SNS, which has an unique user number and will get vaccinated and request the vaccination certificate**|
| **SNS user number** | **Número de utente do SNS** | **Unique number which refers to an unique SNS user**|
| **Time interval** | **Intervalo de tempo** | **Elapsed time between vaccine takes/doses**|
| **Vaccination** | **Vacinação** | **Process of administering vaccines to produce immunity against a disease**|
| **Vaccination center** | **Centro de vacinação** | **Facilities specifically created to administer vaccines of a single type as response to an ongoing disease outbreak**|
| **Vaccination certificate** | **Certificado de vacinação** | **Certificate which proves that a certain SNS user was vaccinated - includes vaccination date, type of vaccine, expiration date and unique code (QR code, number, etc)**|
| **Vaccination process** | **Processo de vacinação** | **Process of administering vaccines to an SNS user**|
| **Vaccination program** | **Programa de vacinação** | **Vaccination plan with the aim of reduce the incidence of, or to eliminate, a particular disease by protecting individuals against that disease**|
| **Vaccine** | **Vacina** | **A vaccine is a biological preparation that provides active immunity to a particular infectious disease. A vaccine typically contains an agent that resembles a disease-causing microorganism and is often made from weakened or killed forms of the microbe, its toxins, or one of its surface proteins.**|
| **Vaccine dosage** | **Dosagem da vacina** | **Each vaccine type and take can have a different dosage, depending on the age group of the SNS User**|
| **Vaccine lot** | **Lote da vacina** | **Specific number or reference which identifies a vaccine**|
| **Vaccine name** | **Nome da vacina** | **For each type of vaccine (e.g. Covid-19), several vaccines exist, each one with different characteristics and processes - Pfizer, Moderna, Astra Zeneca, etc.**|
| **Vaccine type** | **Tipo de vacina** | **Description of the vaccine for a specific disease like: Covid-19, Dengue, Tetanus, smallpox...**|
| **Waiting room** | **Sala de espera** | **Place where SNS users wait for their turn to take the vaccine**|
| **Working day** | **Dia de trabalho** | **Day on which the vaccination center is open from 8 a.m. until 8 p.m.**|
